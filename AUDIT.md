# Audit of the formal core

Date of audit: 2026-10-01. Scope: everything under `agda/src` (32 modules). Companion to `FORMAL.md`
(index of names); this file states what was checked, what each result actually says, where a statement is
weaker than its informal description, and what is not proved.

## 1. What was verified

* Clean rebuild of all 32 modules with Agda 2.8.0.2 and `cubical` v0.9, all under `--safe`: no `postulate`,
  no holes, no termination or positivity pragmas, no `BUILTIN` beyond the library's. Nine modules also pass
  `-WnoUnsupportedIndexedMatch`, which only silences a warning that some functions will not compute on
  transports (as the cubical library itself does); nothing here relies on that computation.
* Every module is imported by `Everything.agda`, so the build checks all of them.
* CI (independent Linux installation) was green on every pushed commit.
* **Non-vacuity.** The parameterized modules (`Sentence`, `Implicit`, `Enumerate`, `Classify`, `Coherence`,
  `Context`, `Category`, `Modes`, ...) are instantiated on a concrete lexicon (`Instantiate.agda`); the typed
  normalizer is *run*: `decEquiv` returns `yes` on `e1;e2` versus `e3` by computation; the critical-pair
  checker accepts a confluent system and rejects a non-confluent one; the finite-model audit validates and
  refutes a declared equivalence.
* **Trusted base:** the Agda kernel and the cubical library (assumed correct), and the translation of the
  informal statements below into Agda types (checked here by reading the statements).

## 2. What is proved, stated precisely

Notation: `Route A B` are chains of declared coercion edges; `r ≈ s` is the congruence generated by the
declared equivalences (an inductive relation with proof-relevant derivations); `Coe A B = Route A B / ≈`;
a *model* gives each type a set and each edge a proposition-valued relation; `⟦r⟧` is the relational
interpretation of a route; `SoundRules M` says each declared equivalence holds in `M`.

**C1 (central).** For all `A B`: `isProp (Coe A B)` iff `∀ r s. ∥ r ≈ s ∥`; `isContr (Coe A B)` iff a route
exists and that coherence holds; and `[r] = [s]` in `Coe A B` iff `∥ r ≈ s ∥`.
`Coherence.coherent⇒isProp`, `isProp⇒coherent`, `contractible⇒exists×coherent`, `exists×coherent⇒contractible`,
`readings-equal`.

**T6.** If `SoundRules M` and `r ≈ s` then `⟦r⟧ = ⟦s⟧` in `M`; hence the interpretation descends to `Coe`.
For sentences: argument-wise equivalent routes give equal sentence meanings.
`Semantics.sound≈`, `routeIndependence`, `interpC`; `Sentence.formSem-indep`.

**T5.** `Coe A B` is a set; composition of classes is well defined, associative and unital; the interpretation
of a composite is the composite of the interpretations (`interpC-∘`). `Category._∘C_`, `assocC`, `idLC`, `idRC`,
`interpC-∘`; congruence `Graph.≈-++-cong`.

**T16 / T1 / T3 / T2.** For the certificate checker on raw certificates: `checkCert c = just f` implies the
raw image of `f` is `c`; every typed sentence is accepted from its raw image; hence typing of raw
certificates is decidable; coercion insertion does not change the surface sentence; an empty route is
rejected exactly when the literal type clashes with the slot type.
`Check.checkCert-sound`, `checkCert-complete`, `decTyped`, `surface-preserved`, `clash-route-rejected`,
`clash-ent-rejected`, `literal-accepted`, `clash-needs-route`.

**T9' / T10 / T11 (modes).** For readings `k : Coe A B`: every reading true implies a given reading true
implies some reading true; a resolved reading entails the reading; the reading of a class equals the Retain
meaning of any representative and Retain does not depend on the representative. A retraction of the
propositional truncation `∥ X ∥ → X` forces `X` to be a proposition. A single referent cannot be of both
senses of a sum; the conjunction of two coercions is refutable when their supports are disjoint.
`Modes.every⇒reading`, `reading⇒some`, `resolve⇒reading`, `reading-rep`, `retain-indep`,
`retraction⇒isProp`, `noChoice`, `homonym-disjoint`, `conjoin-impossible`; `Semantics.Resolve⇒Retain`.

**T12 / T13 / T14 (individuation).** (a) A copredication count that demands distinctness under both coercions
entails the count under each; the same for relational coercions (`Quantified.Three₂→₁`, `Three₂→₂`).
(b) The count under a route does not depend on which equivalent route is used (`QuantifiedRoutes`).
(c) For a functional coercion the relational count equals the count in the image (`Quantified.Functional`).
(d) Counting under `c` equals counting distinct elements of the image, and `X / ker c ≃ Im c` (`Image`).
(e) Countermodel: three books, two works: `Three Book` holds and the count under the content coercion fails
(`Counting.CL15-schema-fails`).

**T8 (context).** Switching coercion edges on or off by a context: routes made of enabled edges mean the same
in the restricted model; routes through a disabled edge mean nothing; if the declared equivalences respect
enabledness, soundness of the rules survives restriction. `Context.restrict-enabled`, `restrict-disabled`,
`restrict-sound`, `Enabled-resp`, `EnabledC`.

**T15 (re-encoding).** Re-encoding referents by equivalences that carry the edge relations along preserves the
meaning of every route and the soundness of the rules (`Reencode`); moreover the two models are *equal* as
models by univalence, so every property of models transfers by transport (`ReencodeUA.model≡`, `invariance`).

**T17 / T18.** A finite model with Boolean edge tables and a passing audit validates every declared equivalence
(`Audit.audit-sound`, `audit-all`); a pair of referents related by one route and not by another proves the
routes are not equivalent and are distinct readings (`Equivalence.apart⇒¬≈`, `apart⇒distinct`).

**T7 (decidable equivalence, normal forms).** Under finite checks on the lexicon (the rule list is finite and
complete; every rule shortens the route; the critical pairs, i.e. overlaps and inclusions of left-hand
sides, are joinable), equivalence of routes is decidable and the readings are exactly the normal forms.
Proved in full: Levi's lemma, the critical pair lemma, termination from length, decidable redex search,
Newman's lemma, Church-Rosser, lifting from lists of edge names to typed routes, and the resulting
`Normalizer`. The critical-pair condition is also checked *inside Agda* by `StringRewriting.Checker.cpDecide`.
`Rewriting`, `StringRewriting`, `TypedFactor`, `NormalizeFull.Build.normalizer`, `Equivalence.decEquiv`,
`classification`.

**T7' (bounded completeness).** Every route within a length bound occurs in the enumerated list of routes;
routes are determined by edge names (decidable equality); a list of representatives that covers the
enumeration and is pairwise apart gives exactly those readings, pairwise distinct.
`Enumerate.routesUpTo-complete`, `routeEq`; `Classify.readings-covered`, `reps-distinct`.

**T4 (fragment).** For predicate sentences with constant arguments: every explicit elaboration is implicitly
well typed (a coercion route exists); every implicitly well typed sentence has an explicit elaboration; if
all coercions are coherent, any two elaborations of one surface are argument-wise equivalent.
`Implicit.elab-sound`, `elab-complete`, `elab-unique`.

## 3. Where a statement is weaker than its informal description

1. **T3.** What is proved is soundness of the *checker* on certificates. Nothing is proved about the Haskell
   elaborator that produces them; that is by design a trust boundary.
2. **T10.** The formal content is the general fact about retractions of the truncation. That this models
   "intentional underspecification cannot be resolved" is an interpretation, not a theorem.
3. **T11.** `homonym-disjoint` is the disjointness of the sum (`inl ≠ inr`); `Conjoin` is a definition.
   The result is correct and cheap; it should not be presented as deep.
4. **T12.** The entailments are projections of a conjunction that was *defined* to demand distinctness under
   both coercions. That this definition is the right truth condition is a linguistic claim, not a formal
   one. The formal contributions are (b), (c), and the countermodel T13.
5. **T13.** It refutes the schema `Three Book ⇒ Three Info` for our abstract formalization (a book type with a
   non-injective content coercion). It is a faithful reconstruction of the issue raised against the
   injectivity axiom, not a statement about the original Coq development, where the axiom is local.
6. **T4.** The uniqueness part assumes `Coherent` (all routes between two types are equivalent), which fails
   exactly for genuinely ambiguous coercions, i.e. where metonymy lives. So the fragment is a consistency
   check with the unambiguous regime of Luo's theory, not a statement about ambiguity.
7. **T7.** (i) Rules must shorten the route, so equal-length equivalences (such as `e1;e2 ~ e3;e4`) cannot be
   oriented by this measure; a different termination argument would be needed. (ii) The critical-pair checker
   uses fuel-bounded greedy reduction: it is sound (a success is evidence) but incomplete (a failure may be
   a lack of fuel). (iii) Decidability is conditional by necessity: equivalence of routes is the word problem
   of a finitely presented category.
8. **T15.** The elementary version is pointwise equivariance; the univalence version is the general transfer.
   Neither says anything about real knowledge bases, only about re-encodings that preserve the edge relations.
9. **T17.** Passing the audit validates the declared equivalences in a *finite model*, not in the real
   knowledge base.
10. **T6, T5.** These are routine (functoriality of relational semantics). They are the necessary foundation,
    not a source of novelty. The less routine results are C1 (as a formulation), T7, T14, T15 by univalence.

## 5. Overall assessment

The formal core is internally sound and non-vacuous: all stated results are machine-checked, the
parameterized development applies to a concrete lexicon, and the central correspondence (C1) holds. The
weakest link with respect to the project's headline is not a missing proof inside Agda but the narrowness
of the certificate: extending it to carry the mode, the chosen reading with its apartness witnesses, and the
individuation criterion is the most valuable next formal step.
